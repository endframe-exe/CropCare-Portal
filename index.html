1. Welcome to the CropCare Information Portal

UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger • Problem Statement #35

Value Proposition: Farmers, agricultural students, and extension learners frequently struggle to locate basic crop-disease symptom guides because advisories, lab manuals, and seasonal circulars are scattered across separate websites and chat groups. CropCare centralizes visual symptom profiles, seasonal monitoring timetables, preventive dosage calculators, and advisory notices onto one responsive, interactive portal.

2. About CropCare — Vision, Mission & Specialist Directory

Module 1 & 2 Foundation

Vision & Mission

  • Vision: Empower rural growers and agricultural learners with clear, responsive visual awareness of staple crop diseases to prevent avoidable harvest losses.

3. Crop Disease Library, Field Manuals & Seasonal Timetable

Showing 3 of 3 disease profiles

3.1 Tomato Early Blight

Pathogen: Alternaria solani (Fungus)

Visible Symptoms: Dark-brown circular spots on older lower leaves forming concentric "bull's-eye" target rings surrounded by a yellow halo.

Preventive Care: Practice 3-year crop rotation, stake plants for airflow, use drip irrigation, and prune infected lower leaves.

3.2 Rice Blast (Leaf & Neck)

Pathogen: Magnaporthe oryzae (Fungus)

Visible Symptoms: Diamond- or spindle-shaped lesions with grayish-white centers and reddish-brown borders on leaf blades and panicle necks.

Preventive Care: Sow certified blast-resistant paddy varieties, split nitrogen fertilizer into 3–4 doses, and maintain 5 cm standing water.

3.3 Wheat Stem & Leaf Rust

Pathogen: Puccinia graminis / triticina

Visible Symptoms: Clusters of raised, powdery orange-reddish-brown pustules erupting through leaf blades and stems.

Preventive Care: Plant rust-resistant wheat cultivars (HD-3226), follow timely November sowing, and remove volunteer wheat plants.

C. 4-Step Field Application Flow

  1. Pre-Sowing Seed Dressing: Treat certified seeds with Trichoderma viride 24 hours before sowing.
  2. Weekly Canopy Scouting: Walk the plot in a "W" pattern twice a week to inspect older lower leaves first.
  3. Visual Symptom Matching: Compare leaf spots against the CropCare Disease Library and Picture Gallery.
  4. KVK Advisory Escalation: Submit the Symptom Inquiry Form below to verify diagnosis before spraying fungicides.
